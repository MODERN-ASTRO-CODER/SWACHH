The project

Turning trash into trade, and waste into wealth.

SWACHH explores a local-first marketplace connected to a responsible material-return system. The aim is to make local shopping easier to discover and responsible recovery easier to repeat.

💡

The problem we're exploring

Small neighbourhood shops can be hard to discover online, while recyclable materials may lack a convenient, well-documented route from households to appropriate recovery partners.

🔁

The proposed solution

SWACHH combines a simple local product catalogue with a proposed return-and-reward workflow. Its effectiveness must be tested with real merchants, customers and a confirmed recycling partner.
