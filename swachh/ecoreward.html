Shop · Return · Recover · Reward

Make the return loop work.

SWACHH explores a practical way to connect local shopping with responsible material recovery. The idea is simple: make returns convenient, verify what is accepted, then provide a transparent reward.

How it works

Four steps. One accountable loop.

Each step needs a real-world process behind it. The website can support the workflow, but it cannot replace collection partners or proof of material recovery.

🛍️

01 · Shop locally

Customers discover products through participating neighbourhood merchants.

📦

02 · Keep eligible items

Only materials listed in the pilot's accepted-material guide are eligible for return.

⚖️

03 · Verify the return

A trained pilot volunteer or partner records the material type, weight and acceptance.

🎟️

04 · Award points

Rewards follow the agreed rules and are funded by a participating merchant or sponsor.

🚚

05 · Handover safely

Collected material goes to a confirmed destination that accepts that material type.

📈

06 · Report outcomes

Track accepted weight, contamination, costs, participation and repeat returns.

Rules before rewards

Trust is part of the product.

For SWACHH to work beyond a demo, the reward system needs clear eligibility rules and a verified route for every accepted material.

✓Never label material “recycled” based only on collection.
✓Do not accept e-waste or batteries without an appropriate authorised route.
✓Keep demo points separate from real discounts or money.
✓Publish pilot results honestly, including rejected material and costs.
