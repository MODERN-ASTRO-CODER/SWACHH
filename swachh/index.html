A QUICK INTRODUCTION

How to use SWACHH

Try the whole shopping and return journey in a few clear steps.

01
🛒

Browse and order

Explore the sample marketplace and add products to your basket. Sign in with a demo account to complete the checkout.

02
🧾

Keep your receipt

Your SWACHH order receipt is saved in this browser and appears in Order History.

03
📸

Return waste

Open Return & Earn, select an item on your SWACHH receipt, and add a photo of the waste from that product. We only accept waste from goods bought through SWACHH.

04
🎟️

Build one bigger voucher

Material and weight set your points. Redeem them together into one voucher that grows with eligible returns.

Prototype note

This is a student project demo. Products, orders, receipts, returns, and rewards are sample data stored in your browser. No real payment, delivery, or waste collection happens.
