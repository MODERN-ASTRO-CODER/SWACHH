Checkout · receipt verification lab

A cleaner checkout, with receipts checked.

Review your demo basket, apply an EcoReward voucher, and test receipt OCR. This prototype reads visible text and flags basic inconsistencies. It cannot prove a receipt is authentic without a trusted shop or payment-system connection.

02

Receipt scanner

Upload a receipt image and let OCR read it.

Use a receipt you are authorised to check. The scanner extracts text, looks for date/total patterns, and flags missing information. It does not access a retailer database, validate a QR code, or certify authenticity.

Choose an image to begin. OCR runs in your browser using a loaded OCR library.

03 · Developer tool

Demo purchase receipt maker

Create a clearly watermarked sample receipt to test the scanner and checkout flow. It cannot produce an unmarked or real proof-of-purchase document.

Every generated receipt is marked “DEMO · NOT A REAL RECEIPT” and is for software testing only.
